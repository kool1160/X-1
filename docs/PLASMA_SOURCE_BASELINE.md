# X-1 Plasma Source Baseline

## On-hand production candidate

- Plasma source: **LOTOS LTP5500DCNC**
- Machine torch: **PlasmaDyn iPT60 / PTM-60 CNC machine torch**
- Required production material range: 10, 12, 14, 16, and 18 gauge mild steel
- Fine-detail priority: 14–18 gauge

## Consumable family

The iPT60/PT60 family supports multiple nozzle ratings suitable for a controlled cut-quality test matrix:

- 20 A nozzle: PlasmaDyn/Tech.Mo part 51318.065
- 30 A nozzle: PlasmaDyn/Tech.Mo part 51318.08
- 40 A nozzle: PlasmaDyn/Tech.Mo part 51318.09
- 50 A nozzle: PlasmaDyn/Tech.Mo part 51318.10
- 60 A nozzle: PlasmaDyn/Tech.Mo part 51318.11; above the LTP5500DCNC continuous output and not a production candidate for this source
- Common electrode family: 52582 / 880224, subject to physical verification against the installed torch

## Initial test direction

- 18 gauge: begin with 20 A and 30 A nozzles
- 16 gauge: begin with 30 A
- 14 gauge: begin with 30 A and 40 A
- 12 gauge: begin with 40 A
- 10 gauge: begin with 40 A and 50 A

These are test starting points, not released cut charts. Final amperage, air pressure, pierce height, pierce delay, cut height, feed rate, kerf, and target voltage shall be established from controlled coupons.

## Verification still required

- Confirm the exact torch body marking: iPT60M, PTM-60, or equivalent PlasmaDyn machine-torch designation.
- Record torch lead length and connector/pinout.
- Photograph and identify installed retaining cap, swirl ring, electrode, nozzle, and shield/standoff.
- Confirm which nozzle family is physically compatible with the installed torch.
- Verify torch-start contacts, Arc OK availability, and raw-voltage polarity/loading on the exact LTP5500DCNC unit.
- Route 1:1 raw arc voltage only through a documented galvanically isolated high-voltage measurement interface.
- Compare controlled LOTOS results against the Everlast 82i and a known mechanized fine-cut process before final source release.
